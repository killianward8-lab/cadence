## Stage 1 · Frame

### Standard  `standard`
1. Hey [name], good to meet you. Where are you calling in from?
2. I'm Killian, I'm on the founding team here at Poppy. I work with business owners on the strategy side of their content.
3. I looked over what you shared on the form, so I've got a bit of context.
4. Here's the plan for today: I'll ask you a bunch about the business and your content, where you're at and where you want to go. Then if it makes sense, I'll walk you through what working together looks like.
5. And if it's not the right fit, no stress, I'll be honest with you either way. Sound good?
6. So what made you book the call?
7. What were you hoping to get out of it?

## Stage 2 · Current State

### Standard  `standard`
1. Walk me through where things are right now.
2. How long have you been doing this?
3. Where are clients coming from right now?
4. Roughly how many clients right now?
5. What's that bringing in a month?
6. What's working right now?
7. What's not?
8. How long has it been like that?

### Their content  `content`

2. How often?
3. Who's making the content?
4. How many clients has content brought you?
5. Mind if we pull up your Instagram together?

2. Who makes the content for them?
3. How long does one client's content take a week?
4. What happens when you take on a new client?

4. Why that number?
5. Why does that matter to you?
6. What changes if you get there?

### Quit the job  `quit`

2. By when?

2. Who's someone who's where you want to be?
3. 0 to 10, where are they?
4. And where are you?
5. What's missing between the two?

### Onboarding  `ob`

2. By when?
3. If Poppy only did one thing for you today, what would make this a no-brainer?

## Stage 4 · Root Cause

### Standard  `standard`
1. What's stopped you from getting there so far?
2. What have you tried?
3. What happened?
4. Why do you think it didn't work?
5. If you know what to do, what's held you back?

### "No time"  `time`
1. What does your week look like right now?
2. Is it the hours, or something else?
3. If you had the hours, would it be done?
4. What's really behind that?

### Tried everything  `tried`
1. What have you tried?
2. Which one came closest?
3. Why didn't it stick?
4. What was missing each time?

### Something heavy  `heavy`
1. What do you mean by that?
2. What happened?
3. (pause, let them talk)
4. What happened after?
5. How long have you felt that way?

### Onboarding  `ob`
1. What's stopping you from posting more right now?
2. So you're at [X], you want [Y], one client is worth [Z], and the block is [pain]. Did I get that right?
3. (Now build THEIR board: their website, their Instagram, one creator they look up to. Never your demo board.)
4. Pull up your website for me, I'll drop it in so Poppy learns your business.
5. Who's one creator you look up to in your space?
6. Pick the hook you like most.
7. Read that script out loud for me. How does it sound in your voice?

### Onboarding  `ob`
1. 1 to 10, how do you feel about Poppy?
2. Why a [number]?

4. (Commitments, one at a time. Wait for the yes.)

7. Can you post 2 to 3 times a week for the next 4 weeks?
